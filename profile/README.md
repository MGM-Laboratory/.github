<!-- =================== ABOUT =================== -->
## <samp>// 01 — Who we are</samp>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="62%" valign="top">

We are **MGM Laboratory** — a research lab working at the
intersection of **Media, Game, and Mobile**. We treat the internet as a
material: something we shape with care, ship in public, and revise often.

Our work spans **web engineering**, **mobile apps**, **interactive media
(XR · VR · AR · games)**, and the **UX & HCI research** that keeps it all
honest. We collaborate with industry partners, mentor student engineers,
and publish a steady stream of internal libraries, tools, and case studies.

> Part of Fakultas Ilmu Komputer, Universitas Brawijaya
